# Geometry of the stone

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `strike_zone_map.png` | top view of the 3D model with the 24 numbered zones; scale 2.8 mm per pixel |
| `zone_coordinates.csv` | centre of each zone and distance to the nearest edge (used by notebook 04) |

**3D model:** photogrammetric capture with Polycam, available at
<https://poly.cam/capture/53E74E72-5304-436D-99F4-4C9103481075>. The slab rests near its centre on other stones and
its edges are free.

`zone_coordinates.csv`: `zone`; `x_m`, `y_m` (m): centre of the zone in the top view. The origin is the lower-left
corner of the rectangle that encloses the outline of the slab in `strike_zone_map.png`; `x` runs along the length of
the slab (to the right in the map, 0–2.40 m) and `y` across it (upwards in the map, 0–1.03 m); scale 2.8 mm per pixel.
`edge_distance_m`: distance from the zone centre to the nearest point of the outline in plan (m). Nineteen zones lie
within 13 cm of the edge; five (Z16, Z17, Z19, Z20, Z22) lie 19–43 cm from it.

**Dimensions:** length 2.40 m and perimeter 6.40 m (measured with tape); width 1.05 m and area ≈ 1.8 m² (map);
thickness ≈ 0.15–0.30 m (3D model). **Location:** 12°37'53.6" S, 76°39'43.0" W (Plus Code 57V5989Q+96), ~144 m above
sea level.
