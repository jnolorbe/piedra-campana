# Elemental composition (XRF)

`xrf_ipen_report_1730.csv`: 14 elements from Report of Results No. 1730 of the Instituto Peruano de Energía Nuclear
(IPEN), issued on 1 December 2025 (X-ray fluorescence; sample ground to < 63 µm; 2 replicates).

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `element` | element analysed |
| `unit` | % by mass or mg/kg |
| `value` | mean of 2 replicates |
| `uncertainty` | standard deviation of the 2 replicates |
| `note` | laboratory remarks (e.g. 20 % difference in Al against the reference material) |

**Sample:** a loose fragment collected next to the slab (no material was removed from the stone), chosen for its
similar colour and roughness; it describes the surrounding rock, visually indistinguishable from the slab. Na, Ca, Mg,
P and loss on ignition were not reported. The original report is not included because it prohibits its reproduction.
