# Photographs and 3D model of the stone · 19 October 2025

Taken on the day of the main session (S2), before the recordings, with the Xiaomi M2101K7AG phone (the one used as
accelerometer). The photographs have no EXIF metadata (location and device data removed); the pixels are unchanged
apart from the orientation.

**3D model:** one textured mesh of the slab and the stones around it (bounding box ≈ 5.2 × 2.3 × 4.3 m, y axis up,
units of the capture; the scale used in the article comes from the tape measurements, see `../geometry/metadata.md`).
It can be opened with any glTF viewer (e.g. Blender, Windows 3D Viewer, macOS Preview/Reality Composer, or
<https://gltf-viewer.donmccurdy.com>). The top view `../geometry/strike_zone_map.png` and the views in Figure 2 of the
article were rendered from this model.
